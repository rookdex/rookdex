## Before you press play

Check that the download finished. A boxed copy holds a download code, not a disc, so it needs the same download. Keep the console connected and install any update it offers first.

## If it won't start

Check the service status page on support.rockstargames.com first. Busy servers right after a launch are normal. Problems with a purchase or a missing download go to the store you bought from (PlayStation, Xbox or the shop), not to Rockstar.

## What you don't need to claim

- **Ultimate Edition items** unlock chapter by chapter as you play the story. There is nothing to hunt for in the first hour.
- **The Vintage Vice City Pack** (a '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage, outfits, hairstyles and a weapon pattern) comes with every pre-order and with every digital purchase before 20 November 2026. You can now also buy it on its own on PlayStation and Xbox, for {price:vintage-pack} in the PlayStation Store.
- **The GTA+ month** comes with a digital copy bought before 20 November 2026. It covers GTA Online, not the story. You claim it in the PlayStation Store (by 31 March 2027) or under Offers & Credits in the Microsoft Store (within 180 days), and it renews at full price until you cancel. With no GTA Online at launch, there's no hurry.

## Playing as two

You play as Jason and Lucia. Some missions lock you to one of them, some make you switch and some let you choose. So don't worry if you can't switch whenever you like.

## When the police are after you

Edge's preview describes a new heat system. Icons at the top of the screen show what the police know about you: your description, whether you had a partner, what you wore, whether CCTV caught you and what weapon you carried. Split up, change clothes or drop the weapon and you match the report less. A newer, expensive car may hide a tracker. A separate Criminal Profile rates how you play, from Professional to Irredeemable.
