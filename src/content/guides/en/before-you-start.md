## The date

The game came out on **19 November 2026** on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It launched without a PC version and without GTA Online.

## Editions

Two editions are on sale: Standard at {price:standard} and Ultimate at {price:ultimate}. Ultimate is the game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: premium vehicles, weapons and clothes for Jason and Lucia across the whole story, with new items unlocked in each chapter. Both PlayStation and Xbox sell it.

## Pre-order bonus

The Vintage Vice City Pack comes with every pre-order and with every digital copy bought before 20 November 2026. You can now also buy it on its own on PlayStation and Xbox, for {price:vintage-pack} in the PlayStation Store.

## GTA+

A digital copy bought before 20 November 2026 includes one month of GTA+. On PlayStation the membership renews by itself until you cancel it, and you have to redeem the month by 31 March 2027. If you don't want to pay for a second month, cancel before it renews.

## The download

The download is up to **212 GB**, so start it well before you plan to play. A physical copy doesn't save you the download: the box holds a download code.

## Buying in Norway

The standard digital edition is {price:standard} on the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store. Physical copies come from the usual Norwegian retailers.
